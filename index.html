FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Add your question to the request form and we'll answer it with your quote.

Which areas do you serve?

We clean homes in Miami and nearby neighborhoods in Miami-Dade County. Include your ZIP code in the form and we'll confirm availability for your address.

How is the price of a cleaning determined?

Pricing depends on the size and condition of your home, the type of service and how often you'd like us to visit. You'll always receive a written quote before your first cleaning.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients prefer to be out. We'll agree on how to access your home when we confirm the appointment.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. Our team brings the products and equipment needed. If you prefer that we use specific products, just let us know.

Are your products safe for children and pets?

We favor eco-conscious, low-odor products and use them carefully. Tell us about allergies, sensitivities or pets in your request so we can plan accordingly.

Can I reschedule or cancel a visit?

Of course. We ask for at least 48 hours' notice so we can reorganize our team's schedule.

What if something was missed?

Let us know as soon as possible and we'll arrange to make it right. Your feedback also helps us refine the checklist for your home.
