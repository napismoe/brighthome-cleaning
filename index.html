Services

Cleaning shaped around the way you live.

Five service concepts, from weekly upkeep to a full reset before moving day, each built around a clear, room-by-room checklist.

  • Standard Cleaning

    Regular upkeep for kitchens, bathrooms, bedrooms and living areas.

    • Dusting of reachable surfaces
    • Kitchen counters, sink and appliance exteriors
    • Bathrooms cleaned and sanitized
    • Floors vacuumed and mopped

  • Deep Cleaning

    A top-to-bottom reset for homes that need extra attention.

    • Baseboards, doors and light switches
    • Inside the microwave, outside the oven
    • Build-up removal in showers and tubs
    • Detailed dusting of fixtures and vents

  • Move-In / Move-Out

    An empty-home clean designed for handing over the keys or settling in with confidence.

    • Inside cabinets, drawers and closets
    • Inside the refrigerator and oven
    • Windowsills and tracks
    • Full floor care, room by room

  • Recurring Cleaning

    Weekly, every-other-week or monthly visits, designed to keep a home consistently fresh.

    • The same checklist on every visit
    • Consistent results over time
    • Simple schedule changes

  • Vacation Rental Turnover

    Guest-ready turnover cleaning, designed for short-term rentals.

    • Linen change and bed making with the host's linens
    • Kitchen and bathroom reset
    • Guest-ready presentation, every time

Why BrightHome

The difference is in the details.

  • Products chosen with care

    The concept favors low-odor, eco-conscious products, used thoughtfully around children, pets and delicate surfaces.

  • Checklist-driven consistency

    Every visit is designed around the same detailed checklist, so results stay consistent over time.

  • Clear, upfront quotes

    Pricing is presented as a written quote based on home size and service type, so expectations are clear from the start.

  • Scheduling that fits Miami life

    Morning, midday or afternoon time windows, with simple rescheduling built into the service design.

  • Attention to small surfaces

    Light switches, handles, baseboards: the details that make a home feel truly clean.

  • Everything in writing

    The proposed service flow keeps every detail written down, from the first quote to the final checklist, so nothing gets lost.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How the BrightHome concept is designed to work, and what this demo does and doesn't do.

Is BrightHome Cleaning a real company?

No. BrightHome Cleaning is a fictional business created for a web design portfolio. No services are offered, and the quote form doesn't send or store anything.

Which areas would the service cover?

The concept is set in Miami and nearby neighborhoods in Miami-Dade County, Florida. As a demo, it doesn't actually serve any area.

How would pricing be determined?

In this concept, pricing depends on the size and condition of the home, the type of service and the visit frequency, always presented as a written quote. No real prices exist for this demo.

Would I need to be home during the cleaning?

The service is designed so you wouldn't need to be. Home access would be agreed in advance.

Are supplies included?

In the concept, yes: products and equipment are included, and preferences for specific products can be noted in the quote form.

What about children, pets and allergies?

The concept favors eco-conscious, low-odor products, and the quote form includes space to mention pets, allergies or sensitivities.

Could visits be rescheduled?

Yes. Simple rescheduling with advance notice is part of the service design.
