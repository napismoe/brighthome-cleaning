Privacy notice

Your entries stay in your browser.

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This website is a portfolio demo. BrightHome Cleaning is a fictional business: it offers no services and does not collect, transmit or store the information you enter in its quote form.

The quote form

The form exists only to demonstrate design and validation. When you use it:

  • Your entries are checked by code running in your own browser.
  • They are not sent to any server, email address or third-party service.
  • They are not saved in cookies, local storage or any database.
  • After a successful check, the form is cleared.
  • No one receives your entries, and no one will contact you.

Even so, please use sample details rather than your real personal information.

Cookies and tracking

This demo does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Third-party resources

The typefaces (Fraunces and Inter) are loaded from Google Fonts. To download them, your browser connects to Google's servers, which receive standard technical information such as your IP address. That connection is covered by Google's privacy policy.

The photographs are served with the site's own files. The links to photographers' Unsplash profiles only open Unsplash if you click them.

Hosting

Like most websites, the service hosting these files may keep standard technical logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested. The demo itself adds no logging or data collection of its own.

Questions

Because BrightHome Cleaning is fictional, there is no company to contact. For questions about this project, please use the contact details on the portfolio where you found it.
